🇬🇧 Privacy Policy — Pétanque Scanner

Last Updated: October 2026
Developer: Vince427
Application: Pétanque Scanner (com.petanquescanner.app)

1. Summary

Pétanque Scanner is a 100% local, offline-first application. It operates without any user account, without requiring an internet connection, and never collects, transmits, or sells personal data to third parties. All your data stays strictly on your device.

2. Data Stored on the Device

Data TypeStorage LocationExternal Transmission
Match scores & historyLocal (Room SQLite)None
Tournaments (teams, players, rankings)Local (Room SQLite)None
Player profile (name, club, city)Local (SharedPreferences)None
App settings (accessibility, voice, measurement mode)Local (SharedPreferences)None
Photos captured for distance scanningIn-memory only (ephemeral)None
Video clips (Clip Mode)App-private local storageNone (unless explicitly shared by you via Android Share Sheet)
Voice commands100% offline on-device processing (Vosk)None

3. Permissions Used

PermissionPurpose
CAMERACapture ground images for on-device OpenCV distance measurement and optional video clips
RECORD_AUDIOHands-free voice commands processed 100% offline via Vosk
FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONEKeep offline voice recognition active during an ongoing match
VIBRATEHaptic feedback on score and measurement interactions
POST_NOTIFICATIONSDisplay active status notification while the voice assistant is listening

4. Offline Voice Recognition & Computer Vision

Voice commands use Vosk (100% offline speech recognition) and distance measurements use on-device OpenCV. No audio or camera frame is ever uploaded to a remote server.

5. Contact

For any privacy questions: petanquescanner@gmail.com

Pétanque Scanner est une application 100 % locale. Elle fonctionne sans compte, sans connexion et ne collecte, ne transmet ni ne vend aucune donnée personnelle à des tiers. Toutes vos données restent sur votre appareil.
